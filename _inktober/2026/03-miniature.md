#### What I learned

I struggled a bit with this one. I was stuck on the idea of misunderstanding which was miniature. I played with a giant shoe, a paw even a chicken's foot as the big element. It got really late in the day and I wasn't happy with any of the trial sketches. I fell back to a cylinder, a giant coffee cup.
